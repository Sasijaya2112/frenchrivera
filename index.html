Our Story

A house with French soul

French Rivera is a boutique guest house in White Town, the French Quarter of Puducherry, where ochre villas, shuttered windows and bougainvillea-lined lanes still set the pace of the day.

Our three-storey villa wears the colours of the neighbourhood: mustard walls, white arches, louvred doors and wooden balconies. Inside you’ll find cool black stone floors, a sunlit staircase, local art and quiet corners to settle into after a day by the sea.
